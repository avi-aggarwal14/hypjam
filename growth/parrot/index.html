02 · What we learned
Where Parrot is today
The product
The TikTok for language learning. Scroll short videos from native speakers, matched to your level. Tap any word in the captions for a translation and save it, then practise: match the words, fill the blank, build the sentence, and say it out loud with feedback on your pronunciation. Built on comprehensible input: understand about 80%, stretch for the other 20%.
The offer
Free to download, with a 7-day free trial on the monthly Parrot+ plan, on iPhone, Android and the web. 4.7 stars from 17,237 ratings on the US App Store. Your 22 October update added a streak calendar, spelling matched to your level and new Polly level cards.
Your own channels
@parrotapp on TikTok: 454K likes, and 1.4M plays on "Learning Spanish so I can jajajaja instead of hahaha". Creator pages that use Parrot: 9.3M on @tommy.learningspanish (5 Oct) and 986K on @annalearns.spanish (9 Oct). Amir on X: 49K views on the Polly launch.
Already in motion
  • Your methodology page republishes the learner count every day: 712,878 on 25 October.
  • Polly, a screen-free plush tutor built on the Parrot Method, is in pre-order.
  • The AI Connector brings a learner's level, streak and words into ChatGPT and Muse.
  • Your team is filming street-interview quizzes on Spanish false friends: calcetines, queso, ventilador.
What that tells us
Parrot travels when a real person shows the feed inside their own Spanish story. Your biggest videos, from your page and from creator pages, follow that pattern. What is missing is volume: more faces, from more kinds of learners, every week.
The timing
January is the category's biggest month. Babbel's US CEO told the BBC that January is "the largest month of the year by far", and Duolingo's annual report says usage rises from late December as people resolve to learn languages. Videos started in November have time to find what works first.

04 · The objection
"Can scrolling really teach me?": the objection to win
Before anyone pays for a feed, they ask two things: will I actually learn, or just watch? And is this the Spanish people really speak? The videos should answer both.
What you already promise
  • Real videos from native speakers in Mexico, Spain, Colombia, Argentina, Peru and more
  • Matched to your level: understand about 80%, stretch for the other 20%
  • Tap any word in the captions, save it, review it with spaced repetition
  • Parrot it back: say it out loud with feedback on your pronunciation
  • 10 to 15 minutes across your day, in the gaps you already scroll
The proof you already have
2.1M lessons completed and 4.7 stars from 17,237 ratings. Your learner stories include Nadia, A0 to B2 over 5,847 lessons, and David, who started Spanish at 60.
The most believable shot
A word tapped and saved in the feed, then said out loud to a real person later that week. The saved-words list next to a real conversation is the shot that answers the objection.
How we use it
Script A ends on a saved word used in real life. Script B takes saved words to a family dinner. Script C asks the same question on day 1 and day 30, and keeps whatever happens.
On camera
Creators show their real level, mistakes included. Partners and family appear only if they are adults and agree first.
What creators never say
No "fluent in 30 days". No promised level or timeline. Nothing your own pages do not say Parrot does.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Parrot" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Parrot themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Parrot does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Real learning on camera
Creators show their real level, real saved words and real mistakes. Nobody promises fluency, a level or a timeline.
People on camera
Partners, family and friends appear only if they are adults and agree on camera first. No children on camera or on mic.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
