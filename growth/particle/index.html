Why creator videos work

People trust people

Show, do not tell

Every Particle story shows how many articles it is built from. A creator opening one story and reading it in a few lines shows the whole product in one shot.
Objections
Answer "I don't have time for the news"

Fuel for ads

04 · The objection
"I don't have time to keep up": the objection to win
Most people want to know what is going on, but feel the news takes too long and asks too much. The videos should answer on screen: one story, many sources, read or heard in the time a creator already has.
What you already say
  • We've built Particle to help people stay informed without feeling overwhelmed
  • Read the news in summarized bullet points that tell you what you need to know
  • The full picture in half the time
  • Hit Play to listen to your personalized feed of news, perfect for your commute
  • Particle reads thousands of articles so you don't have to
The habit already exists
Commute videos, 5am routines and daily puzzles already travel on TikTok, as the next frames show. Each one is a slot in the day that people protect, and Particle fits inside it.
Show the time on screen
Every script makes the time visible: the stops on the train, the timer on the crossword, the minutes before class. The viewer sees how little it takes.
How we use it
Script A presses Play on the commute. Script B reads one story three ways. Script C plays the daily crossword. Script D plays the podcast clip inside a story.
Keep it neutral
Every example story is non-political: a launch, a game, a science find, the weather. Creators read Particle's summaries as they appear, with the sources in view.
What creators never say
Nothing your own pages do not say Particle does. No remarks about other news apps or outlets. No parties, candidates or elections on screen.

How the paid ads would run
1 · Film
Each script is shot once, with 8 different openings.
→
2 · Launch
Each opening runs as its own ad, from the creator's handle as a partnership ad.
→
3 · Read
After a few days we compare cost per download and how many people watch past 3 seconds.
4 · Cut
The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
Your App Store and Google Play listings, with one tracking link per creator, so every download traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run next to your team's), and a weekly look at downloads and at people who come back the next morning.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Particle" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Particle themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Particle does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Non-political, always
Example stories are launches, games, science, space and weather. No parties, candidates, politicians, elections or opinions on screen, and no comments on other news apps or outlets.
Particle in your words
Features shown exactly as the app shows them on the day and described only as your pages describe them. Summaries are read as they appear, with the article list in view.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
