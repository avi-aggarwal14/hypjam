02 · What we learned
Where Tiimo is today
The product
Tiimo, from Copenhagen: "a visual AI planner built for real life." A visual timeline, to-do lists, a focus timer, widgets and Live Activities, Apple Watch, and an AI co-planner that turns what is on your mind into steps with time estimates. On iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, Android and the web.
Recognition
iPhone App of the Year 2025. Apple called it "an impressive visual planner and thoughtfully implemented AI that turns aspirations into actionable next steps." Also a 2024 Apple Design Awards finalist and one of Fast Company's most innovative companies of 2025. 4.6 stars from more than 21,000 ratings on the US App Store.
Just shipped
15 September: ready for iOS 27, with Siri, Intelligent Lists and Snap to plans, which turns a photo of a sticky note into tasks. 21 September: timers on subtasks. 21 October: Google Calendar on Apple Watch. Android, rebuilt in May, now lists the co-planner too.
Already in motion
  • About 13 active US Meta ads, including "Talk it out, and Tiimo turns your thoughts into clear, actionable tasks" and a creator-style video.
  • Creator partnerships on TikTok: @palopenguin's "download Tiimo today" passed 1.9M plays in February, run as an ad.
  • Organic posts keep coming: a Tiimo Q and A reached 23.2K plays in April, and a UK creator's "try out the Tiimo App" 9.6K in January.
What that tells us
Creators already sell Tiimo when they get the chance: the partnership posts travel and people post about it on their own. What is missing is volume: many US creators every week, each video tested as a partnership ad.
The timing
November resets, Thanksgiving on 26 November, the holidays, then the New Year, when everyone plans. "iPhone App of the Year" is also freshest before Apple names its next winners. Videos started now are tested before January.

04 · The objection
"I always quit planners": the objection to win
Most viewers have downloaded a planner before and stopped using it. The videos should answer with a real day that changes, and a plan that keeps up.
What you already say
  • The planner people don't quit
  • Planning becomes something that supports you, not something that drains you
  • Plans changed? Just tell Tiimo
  • Running late or low on energy? Your co-planner reshuffles the day for you
  • No clutter, no pressure
Show it, do not claim it
Every script has a moment where the day changes: a meeting moves, energy dips, a task runs long. The creator tells Tiimo and the timeline updates on screen. A week in, they are still planning in it, because they used it before filming.
Visible without opening it
Widgets, Live Activities and the Apple Watch keep the plan in view all day. Script C makes that the whole video, so viewers see a planner they do not have to remember to open.
How we use it
Script A talks out a day and reshuffles it at noon. Script B plans the week on Sunday and checks in on Wednesday. Script C tours the plan on the Home Screen and the wrist. Script D puts time on screen with the focus timer.
Words that fit your community
Creators describe Tiimo in your words: a visual AI planner built for real life, made by and for neurodivergent people. They speak only for themselves, and anyone who mentions their own ADHD or autism does it in their own words, never as a claim about what Tiimo does for it.
What creators never say
No medical, diagnosis or treatment claims, nothing like "fixes my ADHD", and no stereotypes. No productivity numbers they did not measure. No other app knocked by name. No children on camera.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Tiimo" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Tiimo themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Tiimo does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Honest claims
Creators describe Tiimo in your words: a visual AI planner built for real life. No medical, diagnosis or treatment claims, no "fixes my ADHD", and no promises about focus or productivity. Features are shown only as they work on the creator's own phone.
Neuroinclusive by default
Creators speak only for themselves, nobody is asked about a diagnosis, and nobody speaks for a whole neurotype. Subtitles on every video, no flashing edits, and no jokes at anyone's expense.

Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
