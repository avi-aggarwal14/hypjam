01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
A brand saying "it tracks your sets" is a claim. A lifter finishing a session and opening the app to every set already there is proof.
Show, do not tell
No screen means it needs a demo
Fort has no screen, so the product is the moment after the workout, when the app shows what it caught. A short video shows that in ten seconds.
Objections
Answer "will it catch my sets?" out loud
It is the first thing every lifter wonders. One real session, filmed start to finish, answers it better than any spec sheet.
A reason to act
Batch 1 sold out
Batch 2 is on pre-order now, and a pre-order can be cancelled for a full refund any time before it ships. Creators can say both in the first seconds.
Fuel for ads
Your best ads come from creators
The strongest creator videos become your Meta ads. We test 8 openings per script, so the budget follows what people actually watch.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Gym videos are already the feed
A Fort video looks like a normal workout post, so people watch it instead of scrolling past. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not feel like one.
02 · What we learned
Where Fort is today
The product
The first wearable designed for strength training, in forged stainless steel, ceramic and sapphire crystal, with no screen. Motion sensors detect exercises and sets and estimate rep ranges. You add weight and effort after, and heart rate, daily movement and sleep give the training context.
The offer
Batch 2 is on pre-order and ships Q2 2027. Batch 1 sold out. Each order includes one band and a year of premium software. Pre-orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before they ship, and there are 30-day returns from delivery.
Your own channels
Miranda's "Introducing Fort" video: 480.6K views on X (11 March). "First cast metal prototypes of Fort are in": 52.5K (26 February). "Wearables that look like jewelry": 38.7K (28 July). @fort posted the new website on 24 October.

  • A new website and store, live since 24 October.

  • Fort workout events, women's weightlifting workshops run with Fort, and Fort at Equinox, this summer.

What that tells us

The timing
The new site went live on 24 October and Batch 2 is open. The months before it ships are the window to fill it, and every week of creator videos between now and then is another week of hooks tested and pre-orders traced.

04 · The objection
"Will it catch my sets?": the objection to win
Every lifter's first question is "will it actually know what I did?" The answer belongs in the videos, filmed in one session, not just on the site.
What you already say
  • Motion sensors detect exercises and sets and estimate rep ranges
  • Models built on thousands of hours of data, including 20,000 reps
  • Your lab and beta testers add more every day
  • You review what Fort captured and add weight and effort
Screenless on purpose
Your site says it: keep your attention. No screen to check between sets. The video shows the session first and the app after, which is exactly how Fort is meant to be used.
Pre-ordering is low risk
A full refund any time before your order ships, and 30-day returns from delivery, no questions asked. Said on camera, it makes reserving one now an easy yes.
How we use it
Script A films a full session with the phone in the bag, then opens the app. Script C has a lifter keep their own notes and compare at the end, on camera, whatever the result.
In the gym
Only the creator is identifiable. Other members stay out of frame or blurred, and every shoot follows the gym's own filming rules.
What creators never say
No exact rep-count promises: your site says rep ranges. No health, injury or medical claims. Nothing your site does not say Fort does.
