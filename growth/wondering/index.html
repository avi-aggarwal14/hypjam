01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
An app saying "learn anything" is a claim. A real person explaining quantum mechanics on day 7 is proof.
Show, do not tell
The product is the moment it clicks
A diagram landing, a podcast answering a question mid-walk. A 20 second video can show the moment a hard idea makes sense.
The audience is there
Curiosity already trends
Personal curriculums, "learnings of the week" and anti brain rot posts already pull hundreds of thousands of views. Wondering fits straight into them.
Habit
Streaks are social
Friend Streaks turn learning into something two people do. A creator can start one with a friend on camera and invite followers in.
Fuel for ads
Your best ads come from creators
The strongest creator videos become your Meta and TikTok ads. We test 8 openings per script, so the budget follows what people actually watch.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like a normal post
Learning is already what these creators post, so a Wondering video fits their feed. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not feel like one.

03 · The category
What other learning apps are doing

Owns the daily habit
Short daily lessons and streaks, for languages, math, music and chess. Everyone already knows the format, which is why "Duolingo for anything" lands in one line.
Brilliant
Works with STEM creators
Interactive math, science and computer science. It runs paid posts with creators, for example a labelled post from software engineer @allenvert (150K followers).
NotebookLM
Audio went viral on its own
Students posted its AI podcasts in unpaid videos: 1.4M plays for @itsangelicageorges and 389K for @raine_boww. Proof that people share audio learning when it surprises them.
Microlearning apps
"Replace doomscrolling" sells
Two paid posts with the same line, "replace doomscrolling with microlearning", reached 15.9M and 3.0M plays on TikTok.
Also in play
Imprint (a curated library of visual courses), Nibble, Deepstash and Daybrain, which makes personal audio episodes and has started posting on TikTok.
The opening for Wondering
Everyone else sells a library or one subject. In the posts we read, nobody is showing a course built for one person, on any topic, in three minutes, that also talks back. That lane is open.
Sources: wondering.app/compare, TikTok video pages (plays and paid labels read 19 Oct 2026), and the creators' public profiles.
04 · The objection
Will I stick with it? The objection to win
Everyone has an app they meant to learn with and quit by day three. The answer belongs in the videos, not just the App Store page.
What you already built for it
  • 3-minute lessons that fit a queue or a commute
  • Streaks, streak freezes and Friend Streaks
  • Global review when practice is due
  • Podcasts and live mode for when you cannot look at a screen
How the videos answer it
  • Show day 7, not day 1: Scripts A and B are filmed over a week.
  • Make it social: Script C starts a friend streak on camera.
  • Remove the screen: Script D learns on a walk.
The second question
"Can I trust what an AI teaches me?"
Your answers are real: the AI news course is grounded in trusted sources, the tutor checks understanding, and you can ask follow ups. Creators say so in their own words.
How we use it
Every script ends on something the creator can now explain, checked against the course before it posts. That is the trust moment.
What creators never say
No promises about grades, exams or memory. No "you will be an expert". Nothing your own pages do not say Wondering does.
Sources: wondering.app/docs, the App Store listing and Angelica Kosasih's posts of 24 Sep and 29 Aug 2026 on X.

1 to 7 November

December into January
"Learn something new this year" season. The hooks and creators that won in November get the budget.
What this needs from you
  • Free Pro access for each creator, so they can show podcasts, live mode and video
  • One founder clip a week from Cheng-Wei or Angelica showing a new feature
  • A download link for each creator that shows where each sign-up came from

Creator content takes a few weeks to tune. Start now and the proven hooks are ready before everyone else's New Year budget lands.

16 · Ads
How the paid ads would run
1 · Film
Each script is shot once, with 8 different openings.
→
2 · Launch
Each opening runs as its own ad, from the creator's handle as a partnership ad.
→
3 · Read
After a few days we compare cost per download and how many people watch past 3 seconds.
4 · Cut
The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
Your App Store page and the web sign-up, with one tracking link per creator, so every download traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or we run them from ours), an install link per creator, and a weekly look at sign-ups.
17 · Process
How a campaign runs, step by step
1 · Brief
You tell us the goal, the audience and what can and can't be said about Wondering.
→
2 · Cast
We shortlist creators by niche, location and median views. You pick.
→
3 · Offer
We send each creator the brief, the dates and the rate.
4 · Sign
A written agreement with every creator, covering usage and disclosure.
→
5 · Scripts
One per format: 8 hooks, a shot list, screens to record and a caption.
→
6 · Film and upload
Creators film on their phones and upload to a private folder only we see.
7 · Review
We check every video within 48 hours. Nothing posts unapproved.
→
8 · Post
On the creator's own account, with the ad disclosure and the paid-partnership label.
→
9 · Pay and report
Creators paid within 7 days of going live. You get a report every Monday.
Where creators talk to us
One hypjam Discord for every creator. Each campaign gets a private channel group for chat, scripts and posted links that only its creators and your team can see.
Access
Each signed creator gets free Pro access for the campaign, so they can show podcasts, live mode and video. Nobody shows a feature that is not live yet.
18 · Reporting
What we report, and when
Every video
Views, how many people stay past 3 seconds, average watch time, saves, shares and comments.
Every creator link
A tracking link for each creator, so sign-ups trace back to the video that drove them.
Every Monday
A short report: every video, the hooks that won, sign-ups by creator, and what we change next.
Every month
A review call: spend, sign-ups, cost per result on anything run as an ad, and the plan for next month.
19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Wondering" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Wondering themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Wondering does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Learning claims
No promises about grades, exams or memory. Creators show what they learned, in their own words, and nothing about what anyone else will.
Students
Students are part of your audience, but every creator we cast is an adult. No one films in a school or with anyone under 18.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
