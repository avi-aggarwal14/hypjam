01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
"Every character, a different voice" is a claim. A writer hearing her own heroine speak for the first time is proof.
Show, do not tell
A cast explains itself
Two characters talking in two voices. Nobody needs the idea explained once they have heard it for three seconds.
Objections
Let people hear it
The first question about AI voices is how they sound. A creator pressing play on camera answers it in the viewer's own ears, and your free preview does the rest.
Writers share
Every writer has readers
A writer who posts their own story with a full cast brings their readers along, and the story is theirs to share.
Fuel for ads
Your best ads come from creators
Videos that already work on TikTok can run as Spark Ads and Meta partnership ads, with 8 openings per script, so the budget follows what people actually watch.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like a normal post
So people actually watch it instead of scrolling past. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not feel like one.
02 · What we learned
Where Audora is today
The product
An audiobook studio for the stories nobody's recording. Paste a link from Wattpad, AO3, Reddit or Substack, or upload an epub, pdf, docx or txt. Audora reads the text, finds every speaking part, and gives the narration and each character a distinct voice. You can start listening while the rest of the book is still being made.
The offer
Free preview first. Hear the cast before you decide anything: no account, no card, no commitment. Sign in with Google, Discord or an emailed link to keep your audiobooks in your library. Backed by Y Combinator, F26.
Your roots
"Our roots are in fanfiction," your YC page says: built for readers who already know these characters by heart, with voices that capture their personalities and stay consistent throughout the story. Dhanush built for the fanfiction community long before Audora.
Already in motion
  • Seven performed stories on audora.art, from Dracula and A Scandal in Bohemia to Treasure Island and The Importance of Being Earnest, each with its own cast and a tap-a-line player.
  • A launch demo on YouTube, and Dhanush's launch post on X: "I built Audora to solve a problem I had myself, now the world wants it."
  • Your privacy policy (24 October) already covers the Meta Pixel, so ads can be measured from day one.
What that tells us
The product demos itself in seconds: paste a story, press play, and the characters talk. What is missing is volume: writers and readers showing that moment on TikTok, Reels and Shorts every day.
The timing
Full-cast audio has gone mainstream: Audible's full-cast Harry Potter editions sold over 5 million copies in nine months. Readers already know what a cast sounds like. Audora brings one to any story they love.

04 · The objection
AI voices: the objection to win
Most listeners have not heard an AI-voiced audiobook yet, and 61% say they are willing to try one (APA, 2026). The first listen is the moment that wins them, and your free preview is built for it.
What you already say
  • Every character, a different voice.
  • Hear a free preview before you decide anything. No account, no card, no commitment.
  • Voices that capture their personalities and stay consistent throughout the story.
  • Start listening while the rest of the book is still being made.
How we use it
Every script plays the cast out loud within the first 5 seconds, so viewers judge the voices with their own ears, and every video ends on the free preview.
Show it as it happens
Creators make their audiobook in Audora themselves and film it exactly as it sounds on the day. Nothing is re-voiced or mocked up.
Writers come first
Your terms ask people to use Audora for stories they have the right to read and keep, for their own listening. On camera, creators only play stories they wrote, stories shared with the author's permission, or Audora's own samples.
What creators never say
No comparisons with human narrators or other apps, nothing your site does not say Audora does, and no story played on camera without the right to share it.
Sources: audora.art, Audora's interim terms of use (effective 1 October 2026), the YC company page and the Audio Publishers Association's research facts (2026 consumer survey), read 27 Oct 2026.

10 · Script A
Script A: I heard my own characters talk
The brief

Where: TikTok and Instagram Reels from the creator's account, plus TikTok Spark Ads and Meta partnership ads to audora.art (15s and 30s cuts)

Length: 30-38s master. 30s cut: shots 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8. 15s cut: shots 1, 2, 4 and 8 (hook, disclosure, the cast talking, CTA).

Goal: Sign-ups at audora.art, starting with a free preview

Who films it: A US writer, 18+, with a story of their own posted on AO3, Wattpad or Substack (or a manuscript file they wrote). Filmed at their desk or on the sofa: a phone on a stand for face shots, plus screen capture of Audora. They make the preview off camera first, so the reaction is to a scene they chose.

Script D: Listen with me
The brief

Where: TikTok and Instagram Reels from the creator's account; built for saves, with the best cut as a Spark Ad and a Meta partnership ad

Length: 30-36s, mostly B-roll with voiceover.

Goal: Saves and sign-ups

Who films it: A US reading-vlog creator, 18+, who films cozy routines (crafting, puzzles, cleaning, walks, brain stacks). The story playing is one they wrote, one shared with its author's written permission, or one of Audora's own samples. Voiceover over B-roll is fine.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Audora" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Audora themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Audora does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Stories people can share
On camera, creators only play stories they wrote, stories shared with the author's written permission, or Audora's own samples. Nobody posts another writer's story as audio, and every author is credited by name.
Non-explicit, always
Every story, script and example stays non-explicit. Creators who mainly post explicit content are never cast, and fanfic appears only through stories that fit this rule.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
