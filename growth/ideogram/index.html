04 · The objection
"AI can't spell": the objection to win
Most people's first AI image had garbled letters. The videos should show the words coming out right, up close, and how quickly a single line can be changed.
What you already promise
  • Readable type in every layout (Ideogram 4.5)
  • First at spelling correct text in AI images
  • Every shape is yours to change (Ideogram Vector)
  • Detected words become editable text layers in Studio
Put the words in quotes
Your own guide says it: quote the exact words, mention them early, say where they sit. That is a tip a viewer can copy the same day, and a hook in itself.
Fix it without starting over
When a letter comes back wrong, Studio fixes just that line and leaves the rest of the design alone. Showing that once builds more trust than ten perfect results.
How we use it
Every script types the exact words in quotes on screen and checks them up close. Script A zooms into a real vector, and Script C makes holding up edit after edit the whole show.
Keep it real
Whatever comes back first goes in the edit. Fixes happen on camera, and a best-of is always labelled as one.
What creators never say
No "perfect every time". No "replaces your designer". No claims about trademarks, sales, or anything your pages do not say.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Ideogram" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Ideogram themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Ideogram does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Only their own brands
Creators design only names, products and artwork that are theirs, or that they have written permission to use. No famous logos, characters, lyrics or faces.

Sellers follow each marketplace's own rules for AI-assisted designs, and AI-generated images and video carry the platform's AI label where it applies.

Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
