01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
A studio saying "every model in one place" is a claim. An AI filmmaker showing the canvas their last viral mini-movie came from is proof.
Show, do not tell

Objections

Fuel for ads

Doesn't feel like an ad

04 · The objection
"Another AI video app?": the objection to win
Every AI creator already pays for a tool or two. The first question in the comments is "why this one?", and a creator can answer it in ten seconds.
What creators use today
The creators we screened credit Kling, Seedance, Higgsfield, OpenArt, Dreamina, Luma and more, often several in one video.
What a creator can show
  • Every model, one canvas: the same shot in Seedance 2.5 and Kling 4.0, side by side.
  • Templates: clone a trend in a click.
  • MCP: Claude or ChatGPT making the video through YouArt.
The answer that is yours
A way to earn from the same account: 30% affiliate commission, CPP credits and paid Campaigns, and up to $10K for an Original. All on your Partner Program page.
How we use it
Script B answers "why this one?" with a side-by-side test. Scripts C and D end on the creator's Original Hub, so viewers see where the series lives.
The second objection: is it slop?
Film fans on TikTok are asking people not to support AI films (one 8 October post, 7K plays). The creators who win show the craft: the idea, the shots they threw away, the edit. Every script here shows the making.
What creators never say
No "replace Hollywood" and no "no skills needed". No payout promises beyond your own pages. The AI label on, always.

16 · Ads
How the paid ads would run
1 · Film
Each script is shot once, with 8 different openings.
→
2 · Launch
Each opening runs as its own ad, from the creator's handle as a partnership ad.
→
3 · Read
After a few days we compare cost per sign-up and how many people watch past 3 seconds.
4 · Cut
The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
youart.ai, with one tracking link per creator that lands on Free Mode or the template the video used, so every sign-up traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Access to your TikTok and Meta ad accounts (or we run them from ours), your pixel on sign-up and first purchase, and a weekly look at sign-ups and paid plans.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with YouArt" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use YouArt themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say YouArt does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
AI label
Every AI-made video carries the platform's AI-generated label and says "Made with YouArt" in the caption.
Original work only
Paid creator videos use original characters only: no real person's face or voice without their written OK, and no characters someone else owns. Ad platforms reject both.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
