01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
A founder saying "it makes your calls" is a claim. A nurse on her break showing the text that says "booked Thursday at 9:20" is proof.
Show, do not tell
A text thread needs a demo
There is no app to screenshot. The product is the moment a text comes back with the job done, and a short video shows that in ten seconds.
Objections
Answer "what's the catch?" out loud
Free makes people curious about the catch. A real person explaining how Pally plans to earn, from merchants and never from what it recommends, does more than an FAQ.
Free
Free is a hook when a person says it
No card, no waitlist, no invitation. Creators can say that in the first three seconds, and it removes the reason to scroll past.
Fuel for ads
Your best ads come from creators
You already run creator-style ads on Meta. Creator videos add more faces and more openings to test, so the budget follows what people actually watch.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like a normal post
So people actually watch it instead of scrolling past. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not feel like one.
02 · What we learned
Where Pally is today
The product
A personal assistant you text over iMessage, RCS, WhatsApp or Telegram, with no app to install. It remembers, books, chases and calls, sits in your group chats, and has its own number and email. Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Notion, LinkedIn, X and more connect.
The offer
Free, for everyone. No card, no waitlist, no invitation, and almost unlimited usage. Pally plans to earn a fee from merchants when you buy through it, and merchants can never pay to change what it recommends.
Your own channels
Haz's "Pally is now completely FREE" post: 64.3K views and 622 likes. His SF party video: 105.5K. Launch Week Day 2, flights with refunds if the price drops: 74.1K. @pally posted five feature videos between 16 and 22 October.

04 · The objection
"What's the catch?": the objection to win
Before anyone hands an assistant their texts, they ask two things: if it is free, what is the catch, and what can it see? The videos should answer both.
What you already promise
  • Free, with no card
  • It checks before it sends, books or buys anything you have not asked for
  • It never messages anyone without asking
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • See, edit or delete everything it knows
How Pally earns
Your answer is short and believable: a fee from merchants when people buy through Pally, and merchants can never pay to change what it recommends. Said on camera, it turns "what's the catch?" into a reason to try it.
In the group chat
Pally joins only after a private yes, speaks when someone says its name or replies to it, and sends anything sensitive to your own chat. That answers "will it post my stuff in the group?"
How we use it
Script A shows the private yes before Pally joins the group. Script B shows it checking before it books. Script C has the creator say how Pally makes money, in one line, in their own words.
On camera
Friends in a group chat agree before their names or messages are shown. Calls appear as Pally's text summary, never as a recording of the person on the other end.
What creators never say
Never "it does everything" or "you'll never forget anything again". No health or money advice. Nothing your site does not say Pally does.

→

→

4 · Cut

→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
A link or first-text keyword per creator that opens a text to Pally, so every new user traces back to the video that drove it. They run next to the Text Pally ads you already have.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Access to your Meta ad account (or we run it from ours), a way to see new users by link or keyword, and a weekly look at the numbers.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Pally" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Pally themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Pally does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Group chats and calls
Everyone in a group chat agrees before their name or messages appear. Calls are shown as Pally's text summary, never as a recording of the person on the other end.
Work and patients
Nurses film at home, on breaks or on the commute, never on a ward or near a patient. No health or money advice from anyone.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
