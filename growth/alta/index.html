04 · The objection
"I'd never upload my whole closet": the objection to win
Every digital closet hears the same question first: who has time to photograph every piece? The videos should show setup taking minutes, not explain it.
What you already say
  • Closet Scan: hold items up to the front camera and Alta loads them in real time
  • About 100 items in 5 minutes, 200 in 10, 300 in 15
  • Prettify removes the background and smooths wrinkles and shadows
  • Items are tagged by dress code and season
  • Or forward your shopping receipts, add from the Alta database, or take a photo
Film the scan
The scan is the video. A creator holding up pieces one by one while the closet fills on screen is satisfying to watch, and it answers the question in seconds.
Then the payoff
Every script ends on an outfit, not a setup screen: the avatar in tomorrow's look, the real outfit in the mirror, and the least-worn piece finally back in rotation.
How we use it
Script C is the closet scan, start to finish. Scripts A, B and D open on the outfit and show setup in one quick cut, so every video answers the question without stopping for it.
Keep it personal
Only pieces the creator owns go in their closet, and each creator shows only their own avatar, made from their own photos. Outfits, never bodies: no size or weight talk.
What creators never say
Nothing your own pages do not say Alta does. Times and item counts on screen are the creator's own real ones. No prices, savings or shopping totals.

Only the creator's own clothes and their own real trip. Public places only at the destination, no strangers' faces, and nobody else on camera without written consent. No airline, hotel or price talk. Every outfit is one Alta actually suggested from the creator's own closet on the day, shown exactly as the app shows it; never mock up an outfit, an avatar or a list. No prices, savings or shopping totals, and no brand claims for anything on screen. Adults only (18+); nobody else on camera unless they have agreed in writing.

→

The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→

Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→

New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
Your App Store and Google Play listings, with one tracking link per creator, so every download traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.

Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run next to your team), and a weekly look at downloads and closets created.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Alta" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Alta themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Alta does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Avatars, not bodies
Creators show only their own avatar, made from their own photos. No talk about body size or weight, no before and after, and no "flattering". Outfits and avatars appear exactly as the app shows them on the day.
Real pieces
Every item on screen is in the creator's own closet or in your app on the day it is filmed. Brand collections, like Zimmermann in your Discovery tab, appear only as your app shows them, with no prices.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
