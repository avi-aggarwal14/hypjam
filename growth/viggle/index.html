01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
A template preview shows what Viggle can do. A dog owner filming the dance herself, then showing her own dog doing it, shows a viewer it can be their dog next.
Show, do not tell
The before and after is the demo
Viggle's moment is a still photo starting to move. A short video shows the photo, the clip and the result in ten seconds.
Objections
Answer "I can't animate" on camera
Most viewers assume this takes editing skills. A creator who has never animated anything doing it in one take answers that faster than any tutorial.
A real moment
Holiday meme season is next
JST-2 launched on 19 October, and Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year are natural meme moments. Creators showing Viggle now give people a reason to make theirs this year.
Fuel for ads
Creator videos become your ads
Each creator video doubles as a partnership ad. Eight openings per script means the budget goes where viewers actually stay.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like a normal meme
So people watch it twice and send it to a friend. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not interrupt.
02 · What we learned
Where Viggle is today
The product
Meme Maker on the web and in the iPhone app: Mix puts one photo into a motion from 8,000+ templates or a clip you bring, the multi-character editor swaps several people in one scene, Mic and Rap add lip-sync, Live swaps characters in real time on a webcam, and Look Lab transforms a photo. Motion capture maps a clip you record onto any character. PINOC, Viggle Games and the API run on the same models.
Just shipped
19 October: JST-2, your physics-native world foundation model: text, image or video in, 3D characters, motion and scenes out, live in PINOC. 20 October: JST-2 in the API. 17 October: private beta spots for Viggle Animate 2. 26 October: version 2.1.0 of the iPhone app.

50M+ people use Viggle and 300M+ videos have been made with it, from your About page. Your Discord: 4.1M members. TikTok: 170.3K followers and 1.4M likes. Your JST-2 post on X passed 1.7M views. The iPhone app: 4.8 stars from 28.9K US ratings.

04 · The objection
"I can't animate": the objection to win
Most people think a dancing cartoon of their dog takes software and skill. The videos should show it takes one photo and one clip they filmed themselves.
What you already promise
  • One photo and one tap, no editing skills needed
  • 8,000+ templates, or bring your own clip
  • Record yourself dancing and Viggle maps the motion frame by frame
  • Make a character talk, sing or rap with lip-sync
Show the input first
Every video opens on the raw ingredients: the photo of the dog, the drawing, the clip of the creator dancing. Then the result. The before is what makes the after easy to believe.
Their moves, their character
With motion capture the creator's own dance drives the character. The creator trying the dance first, well or badly, is part of the joke and part of the proof.
How we use it
Scripts A and B film the creator's own clip first and show it side by side with the result. Scripts C and D show every selfie going in before the dance plays.
Consent on camera
Every face is the creator's own or an adult friend who says yes on camera. Pets are the creator's own, and so are the drawings.
What creators never say
No "put anyone in it". No celebrities, no real people who did not agree, no copyrighted characters. Nothing your own pages do not say Viggle does.

16 · Ads
How the paid ads would run
1 · Film
Each script is shot once, with 8 different openings.
→
2 · Launch
Each opening runs as its own ad, from the creator's handle as a partnership ad.
→
3 · Read
After a few days we compare cost per download and how many people watch past 3 seconds.
4 · Cut
The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
The App Store and viggle.ai, with one tracking link per creator, so every download traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run alongside your team), and a weekly look at downloads.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Viggle" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Viggle themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Viggle does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Consent for every face
Only the creator's own face, their own pets, their own drawings, and adult friends who agree on camera and in writing. No celebrities, no real people without consent, no copyrighted characters, and no children.
Templates and sound
Templates come from Viggle's own library or a clip the creator filmed. Music comes from each platform's commercial library.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
