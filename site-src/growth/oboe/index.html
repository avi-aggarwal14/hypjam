01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
Your App Store page calls Oboe a better way to spend your time. A creator showing the puzzles they solved instead of scrolling, on their own phone, is the proof.
Show, do not tell
The swipe is the demo
Oboe is a feed, so the product is the swipe: one puzzle solved, the next one already there. A short video shows that in the first two seconds.
Objections
Answer "is it just homework?"
Viewers expect anything good for the brain to feel like work. A real reaction as a Pipes board fills up answers that faster than any line of copy.
Made for the feed
Meet people mid-scroll
Oboe replaces mindless scrolling, and the people it is for are scrolling right now. An "instead of scrolling" video from a creator reaches them at that exact moment.
Fuel for ads
Creator videos become your ads
Each creator video doubles as a partnership ad. Eight openings per script means the budget goes where viewers actually stay.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like a normal post
So people watch it to the end instead of scrolling past. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not interrupt.

04 · The objection
"Brain games feel like homework": the objection to win
Most people expect anything good for the brain to feel like work. The videos should answer with the creator's real reaction: the swipe, the solve, the next puzzle, the same pull as any other feed.
What you already say
  • A better way to spend your time
  • Endless puzzles in one feed
  • Games that sharpen your mind
  • A new type of scroll that is just as satisfying but way better for your brain
  • Every scroll is designed specifically for you
Your own screenshots
Every puzzle is one screen with Rules and Reset, and a status line that reads like a score: "7 left. No rule breaks." or "All 20 full in 24 turns, the fewest." In a video, that line is the caption.
One swipe, one puzzle
Every script shows at least one swipe to the next puzzle, so viewers see for themselves that the feed keeps going, the way any feed does.
How we use it
Script A swaps the scroll for the feed. Script B turns one solve into a score to beat. Script C rates the morning's puzzles one by one. Script D fits three puzzles into one stop.
Keep it honest
Creators say what your pages say: a little smarter every day, a few minutes every day. No promises about IQ, memory, focus or grades, and no screen-time numbers they did not measure on their own phone.
What creators never say
Nothing your own pages do not say Oboe does. No knocking other apps or platforms by name. No children on camera, and no classroom settings.

06 · The moment
From launch week to New Year habits
The all-new Oboe launched on 3 November, Thanksgiving travel and exam season fill the next six weeks, and the New Year is a natural moment to change a habit. Videos filmed in November are tested before that.
Launch to Thanksgiving (26 Nov)
6 to 8 creators who post about scrolling less film Script A, and puzzle creators film Script B. The first partnership ads go live on TikTok and Meta.
Black Friday (27 Nov) to the holidays
Travel and commute creators film Script D at gates and on trains, daily puzzle creators film Script C, and budget moves to the hooks that bring installs.
January
The best swap videos keep running as ads into New Year habit season, and the creators who drove the most installs film a second round.
What this needs from you
  • Oboe on each creator's own phone a few days before filming, so every solve is real
  • A tracking link per creator to the App Store and Google Play
  • A short list of the puzzles creators may show, so videos only show what ships
  • One person on your team who signs off within 48 hours
Why not wait for January?
Launch-week videos are the first thing new players see. Videos filmed in November are tested and cut by December, so the New Year budget backs proven hooks, not first drafts.

08 · Formats
Four formats for Oboe
A · Installs
Swap the scroll
At the moment they would normally scroll, in bed or on the couch, the creator opens Oboe instead and plays through the feed: a Takuzu, a Sliding tiles, a Pipes, joined by the swipe. Cut into 15 and 30 second partnership ads.

B · Reach
Beat my score
One puzzle, one result: the creator solves Pipes or Sliding tiles on camera, reads Oboe's own status line out loud, and dares the comments to beat it. Every stitch brings a new solver.
