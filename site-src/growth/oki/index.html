02 · What we learned
Where Oki is today
The product
Oki Home, a memory computer for your digital life. Photos, files, trips and messages on one timeline, found by asking in your own words. A 27B model runs on the box (RTX 5060 Ti, 16 GB), storage lives on a swappable Memchip from 2 to 16 TB, and there is no cloud subscription.
The offer
Batch 1 at $1,799 sold out and ships mid-December. Batch 2 is $2,299 with a 2 TB Memchip, reserved for a refundable $59, shipping Q1 2027 to the US first. The Mac and Windows apps are out; iOS and Android are in development.
Your own channels
Luofei's launch post: 1.11M views from 2,280 followers, plus follow ups at 145K and 77K. @okihomeai: 304 followers. LinkedIn: 2,364. Facebook: 1.6K. About 29 Meta ads, all from the brand page. No Instagram, TikTok or YouTube account we could find.
Already in motion
  • Batch 1 sold out within six days: Luofei posted it on 12 October.
  • AI accounts on X with up to 302K followers wrote threads about the launch, the biggest at 152K views.
  • Meta ads running since 8 October.
  • Backed by Y Combinator (W25) and MiraclePlus.
What that tells us
The demand is there: one founder post did about 490 times its follower count and sold out a batch. What is missing is faces: people showing their own photos and files in Oki, on the platforms where buyers research a $2,299 computer.
The timing
Ghost launched its $3,499 Core the day before you, and Hark Pro launched the same day. Buyers are comparing personal AI right now, and the first real-life videos will shape how they choose.

04 · The objection
Why not a Mac mini? The objection to win
At $2,299, every buyer asks the same thing: why not a Mac mini, a NAS or the cloud? The answer belongs in the videos.
Your answer, in your words
Luofei, replying under the launch: "not just a chatbot, but a whole auto updating / syncing data store too! this could easily replace cloud drives and other services". That is the line to show, not tell.
What you already promise
  • The model runs on Oki Home, 24/7
  • No cloud subscription
  • Updates only with your approval
  • Your memory on a Memchip you can hold
  • A Linux environment: your hardware, your code
Say what connects
Your Q&A says remote access, connected services and optional cloud models use the internet. Saying that on camera earns more trust than "nothing ever leaves".
How we use it
Script C does the subscription math with real bills. Script D tests the 27B model honestly. Script B shows what a NAS cannot do: describe a memory and get the photo.
Whose data is on screen
Creators search only their own photos and files. Nobody else's messages or faces without their OK, and no children on screen.
What creators never say
No "never lose anything again". No speeds or specs your site does not publish. No "nothing ever leaves the box".
