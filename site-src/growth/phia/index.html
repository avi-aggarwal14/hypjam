Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people

Show, do not tell
The checkout is the demo
Phia's moment happens right before someone pays: one tap on Should I Buy This?, the same piece on other sites, the coupon applied. A short video shows that in the first five seconds.
Objections
Answer "I already have a coupon trick"

A real moment

Black Friday is 27 November and Cyber Monday 30 November. Price-drop alerts and coupons are what people look for that week, so creators showing them give viewers a reason to install Phia before the sales start.
Fuel for ads

Doesn't feel like an ad

04 · The objection
"I already have a coupon extension": the objection to win
Most shoppers already have a coupon trick. What they do not have is the same piece, found new and secondhand, before they pay. The videos should show the find, not just the code.
What you already say
  • Never overpay again
  • Phia compares prices across 220,000+ sites in real time, new and secondhand
  • Should I Buy This? tells you if the price is fair and finds alternatives across retail and resale
  • Not another coupon app: built for people who care about what they wear
  • Shop your screenshots with Phia Lens
More than a code
A coupon is one line at checkout. Phia shows the same piece on other sites, new and secondhand, and watches the one you are waiting on. In a video that is one sentence: "I checked before I paid."
One find per video
Every script ends on a decision, not a feature list: bought it secondhand, waited for the alert, or styled what was already in the closet. Phia is the reason, and the find is the story.
How we use it
Script A opens on a checkout page and ends on the secondhand find. Script B films Black Friday week with price-drop alerts. Script C fills the closet. Script D starts from a screenshot.
Keep it shopping
Phia Rewards are shown as points for gift cards, never as money. No savings totals unless your own pages state them, and every listing on screen is real on the day it is filmed.
What creators never say
Points are never described as money: Phia Rewards are points on eligible purchases, redeemed for gift cards. Authentication is described the way your FAQ describes it, through each resale partner. Nothing your own pages do not say Phia does.

06 · The moment
From Black Friday to the January sales
Black Friday is 27 November, Cyber Monday 30 November, and holiday gifting runs to the end of December. Videos filmed in November are tested before the biggest shopping weeks of the year.
Now to Black Friday (27 Nov)
6 to 8 resale and deal creators film Script A, the find at checkout, and Script B, the wishlist on watch. The first partnership ads go live on Meta and TikTok.
Cyber Monday (30 Nov) to the holidays
Gift shopping: creators check gifts with Should I Buy This? and film Script D from screenshots, and budget moves to the hooks that win.
January
New year, new closet: outfit creators fill their digital closet on camera (Script C), while the best checkout videos keep running as ads into the January sales.
What this needs from you
  • Each creator using Phia on their own phone and laptop for a week before filming, so every find is theirs
  • A tracking link per creator to the App Store and the Chrome Web Store
  • A short list of the features and resale partners creators may show, so videos only show what ships
  • One person on your team who signs off within 48 hours
Why not wait for Black Friday?
People install a price tool before the sales, not halfway through them. Videos filmed in early November are tested and cut by Black Friday week, so the budget backs proven hooks, not first drafts.

Your App Store listing and the Chrome Web Store, with one tracking link per creator, so every install traces back to the video that drove it.

Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.

Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run next to your team), and a weekly look at installs and first checkouts with Phia.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Phia" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Phia themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Phia does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Points, not money
Phia Rewards are shown the way your App Store page describes them: points on eligible purchases at participating retailers, redeemed for gift cards. Points are never called money, cash or income, there are no savings totals unless your own pages state them, and there is no finance talk.
Real listings
Every listing on screen is real on the day it is filmed, on a site Phia showed the creator. Resale pieces are shown with the condition the listing states, and authentication is described the way each resale partner describes it.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
