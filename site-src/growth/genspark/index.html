04 · The objection
"I already have a chatbot": the objection to win
Most people picture AI as a chat window: you ask, then copy, paste and format the answer yourself. Genspark's promise is the finished file. The videos should answer with a demo: one prompt typed on camera, then the deck, the doc and the sheet opened one after another.
What you already promise
  • Just prompt it, and let Genspark handle the rest (your App Store page)
  • You state your intent, and we deliver finished work (Eric, 20 November 2025)
  • AI Slides creates boardroom-quality decks from a prompt, and AI Docs drafts professional docs
  • Media Studio: give a few prompts, and get back a finished piece you would be glad to publish
The habit already travels
An animated website built in under 5 minutes with one prompt passed 280.4K plays, a full-length video from a single prompt 327.1K, and GNUTech's all-in-one Genspark video 118.3K.
Files, not a chat
That is a shot, not a sentence: the prompt typed once, the creator gets on with packing an order or studying, and the finished files open on screen when they come back.
How we use it
Script A ends with four finished pieces on screen. Script B opens the proposal, the deck and the calendar in order. Script D flips through flashcards made from the creator's own notes.
Keep it real
Real prompts typed once on camera, the creator's own shop, client brief or notes, and every file shown exactly as Genspark returned it.
What creators never say
No "it does my homework". No income, sales or grade promises. Nothing your own pages do not say Genspark does.

06 · The moment
Black Friday, finals and the year-end rush
Black Friday is 27 November and Small Business Saturday the 28th, so shops build their holiday launch in mid-November. Finals arrive in December, and freelancers wrap up the year's client work. Everyone has a pile of deliverables at once, which is what one Genspark prompt is for.
Now to Black Friday (27 Nov)
6 to 8 shop owners film Script A, one prompt for the whole holiday launch, and freelancers film Script B. The first partnership ads go live on Meta and TikTok.
Cyber Monday to finals
Students film Script D with their own notes as finals prep starts, and Script C day vlogs run alongside. Budget moves to the hooks and creators that bring sign-ups.
The year-end
Freelancers close out the year with client recaps and new proposals in Script B. The best clips from November keep running as ads into January.
What this needs from you
  • A Genspark account for each creator, on the plan the scripts need
  • A tracking link per creator to genspark.ai, the App Store or Google Play
  • Which products can lead in paid videos: Super Agent, AI Slides, AI Docs, AI Sheets or Media Studio
  • One person on your team who signs off within 48 hours
Why not wait for January?
Shops build their Black Friday launch in mid-November and students start finals prep in early December. Videos filmed now are tested and cut by then, so the budget backs proven hooks when people are busiest.

"Ad | Paid partnership with Genspark" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Genspark themselves before they talk about it.

Nothing your own pages do not say Genspark does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
