A growth breakdown for Fitbod
Why we made this

Fitbod has planned lifters' sessions since 2015, and 4.8 stars from 289K US App Store ratings say people trust the plan. This summer your data team showed something every lifter should hear: the people who got strongest in their first 14 weeks did not train harder, they just kept showing up between weeks four and ten.

This board is one idea for the next ten weeks: everyday lifters on TikTok and Reels filming the session Fitbod picked for them, from Black Friday to the first weeks of January. It is free, so take whatever is useful.

02 · What we learned
Where Fitbod is today
The product
A strength training app that builds each session around your goals, level, equipment and training history. It picks the exercises, sets, reps and weights, updates the plan as you improve, and uses your recovery to suggest which muscles to train next. 1,000+ exercises with multi-angle videos, a Gym Profile for each place you train, and it works with Apple Watch, Apple Health, Strava and Fitbit, on iPhone and Android.
The track record
In the App Store since September 2015. 4.8 stars from 289K US ratings, an Apple Editor's Choice, and 15M+ downloads by your home page's count. Version 8.38.1 shipped on 3 November.
Already in motion
  • About 790 active Meta ads in the US. Of the first 441 by impressions, about two in three run as partnership ads with creators like ArielYu.Fit, Coach Rambo and Derrika Denise.
  • On Instagram, coaches post follow-along workouts with Fitbod, including two labelled "Ad | @fitbodapp" on 24 and 27 October.
  • Your data team publishes studies from millions of logged workouts: how strong lifters are, who keeps getting stronger, and when habits break.
What that tells us
Creator videos already carry Fitbod in paid. What is missing is volume on TikTok and Reels: everyday lifters, not only coaches, filming the session Fitbod picked for them, every week.
The timing
Black Friday is 27 November, and you are pitching Fitbod for Families, one plan for up to five people, as a holiday gift. January brings the year's new lifters, and your own study says weeks four to ten decide who keeps going.

04 · The objection
"Can an app know what I should lift?": the objection to win
Lifters trust their own split, their coach or a routine they saved. The videos should show why a plan that adapts is easier: the weight, the reps and the next session already worked out.
What you already say
  • Know exactly what to do next, without second guessing what is best for you
  • Appropriate weights, reps and sets, with expert demos and cues
  • Workouts built on your equipment, schedule and preferences
  • Your training history and recovery pick the muscles for each session
  • Adaptive AI that learns from your edits
Your own proof
Your study of nearly 19,000 lifters found 97% of sessions use Fitbod's generated workouts, and the lifters who gained the most were the ones who kept showing up between weeks four and ten.
One session at a time
Every script follows one real session from the plan to the last set, and ends on what Fitbod suggests next. The creator's own plan and numbers, never staged.
How we use it
Script A opens on today's plan before the first lift. Script B builds a first session for someone new. Script C rebuilds a session for a hotel gym. Script D reads one of your findings out loud.
Keep it about strength
Progress is shown the way Fitbod shows it: weights, reps and sessions logged. No weigh-ins, no before and after, and no talk of fat loss or bodies.
What creators never say
No weight-loss promises, no injury or medical claims, no supplements, and nothing about other people's bodies. Nothing your own pages do not say Fitbod does.

Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.

Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run next to your team), and a weekly look at installs and trial starts.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Fitbod" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Fitbod themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Fitbod does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Strength, not bodies
No weight-loss promises, no before and after, no weigh-ins, and no talk of fat loss or anyone's body. No supplements, medicines or alcohol in any video.
Safe and real
Every session on screen is the creator's own plan from Fitbod that day, shown as Fitbod showed it. Weights the creator can lift with good form, no max attempts, and no injury, pain or medical claims.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
