01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
Your launch says Gamma 5 asks questions first. A recruiter pasting in a real job description, answering the agent and getting a 30-60-90 day plan back is proof.
Show, do not tell
The magic is the first draft
Gamma's moment is an outline turning into a designed deck, then one sentence changing the whole look. A short video shows that faster than any feature list.
Objections
Answer "is it still my work?" on camera
Job seekers want the deck to sound like them. A creator who starts from her own notes and shapes the outline herself answers that better than any FAQ.
A real moment

Fuel for ads

Doesn't feel like an ad

04 · The objection
"It has to be my work": the objection to win
In a job hunt the deck is a sample of you. The videos should show the thinking coming from the creator: her notes, her answers to the agent, her edits, with Gamma doing the design.
What you already promise
  • Starts with a conversation, outlines first, so you shape the story
  • The agent asks questions and keeps context while you edit
  • Paste in notes or import a PDF, Word doc or webpage
  • Ask, drag, or go fully freeform
Start from your own notes
Your Creation Index found that creations starting from people's own notes, documents, decks or transcripts rose 68% in a year. That is the habit to film: start from the job description, the resume or the list of wins.
Shape it, then show it
Script A stops on the outline so the creator can reorder it out loud. That one beat shows the plan is hers, before a single slide appears.
How we use it
Every script starts from something real the creator brings, and shows at least one change made by asking the agent and one made by hand.
Keep it real
The first draft goes in the edit as it came. Changes happen on camera, and any example role or example win is labelled as one.
What creators never say
No "this got me the job". No "nobody will know". No claims about offers, salaries, promotions or anything your pages do not say.

The brief

Where: TikTok and Instagram Reels from the creator's account, plus 15s and 30s Meta partnership ads pointing at gamma.app

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Gamma" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Gamma themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Gamma does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Real people, real permission
No real candidate's, colleague's or employer's private details on screen. Roles and wins taken from comments are anonymised, and any example is labelled as an example.
No outcome promises
Nobody says a deck got them hired or promoted unless it is true for them. Creators speak from their own experience, never for an employer.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
