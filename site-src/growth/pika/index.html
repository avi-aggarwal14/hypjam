04 · The objection
"I'm not a video person": the objection to win
Many people in ChatGPT have never made a video, and many expect AI video to mean prompts, models and settings. The videos should show how little it takes.
What you already promise
  • Models named when you want them, invisible when you don't
  • Apps tuned by your own creative team
  • Mention @Pika in your chat and start creating
  • Direct the edit in plain language, on a timeline
  • Everything saves to your Pika library
Apps, not prompts
Video Studio, Product Shot, Camera Director and Podcast Video each start from one plain ask. Your own line says it best: the best models, minus the model homework.
Start from what is in the chat
Your launch post shows it: build a product video from the Figma file and the press release already in the conversation. A creator never starts from a blank box.
How we use it
Script A films a first-timer's very first @Pika mention, unedited. Script B starts from a phone photo. Scripts C and D start from an idea said out loud, never a long prompt.
Show it honestly
The first result is the one on screen. Any wait is cut and labelled, and no picked or edited take is passed off as a first try.
What creators never say
No "anyone can be a filmmaker overnight". No speed, price or quality claims your pages do not make. No apps or models that are not live on the day.
