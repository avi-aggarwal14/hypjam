04 · The objection
Will it get my accent? The objection to win
Every UK viewer asks the same thing first: will it understand me? Your UK users already answered it. The videos should too.
Your UK users, in their words
"I dictated this and it understands my Scottish accent. We Scots are traumatised by voice recognition technologies!"

"Now I've come on board with Wispr Flow and they were so on point picking up my English accent."
Why it lands in the UK
Burnistoun's lift sketch, two Scots shouting "Eleven!" at a voice-controlled lift, has 3.76M views on one YouTube upload. The UK has been joking about this for years. Flow gets to answer the joke.
What you already promise
  • Built to handle a wide range of accents
  • British English as a language
  • Learns your names and words
  • 100+ languages
How we use it
Script A puts the accent first: a fast, very local message, then the clean text on screen, unedited. Every other script keeps the creator's own voice and accent.
Show it honestly
Creators pick British English, as your FAQ suggests choosing the one language you are speaking. The first take is the take: no retake passed off as a first try.
What creators never say
No "it never gets a word wrong". No accuracy numbers your pages do not publish. Nobody mocks an accent: creators test their own.
