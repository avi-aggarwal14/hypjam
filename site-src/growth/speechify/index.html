04 · The objection
"Does listening count?": the objection to win
BookTok already argues about it: is listening really reading? The videos should answer with a demo: the voice reading, the words highlighted, at the speed you choose.
What you already promise
  • Every word highlighted in sync with the voice, so you can read along
  • Listen at any speed
  • 100+ natural voices to choose from
  • Snap a photo of any printed page and hear it
The debate already travels
"What speed do you listen to audiobooks on?" passed 55.6K plays, "I listen to audiobooks at 1x speed and apparently I'm the minority" 67.6K, and one creator's reaction to listening faster than 2x 337.4K.
Read along, on camera
Your App Store page puts it simply: the text is highlighted word for word so you can read along. That is a shot, not a sentence: the page, the voice and the highlight moving together.
How we use it
Script D runs the debate on camera: one page at 1x, 2x and 3x with the highlight on. Scripts A and C show people reading along as they listen.
Keep it real
Speeds and voices exactly as set in the app on the day, and the creator's own book or reading, with only a few lines on screen at a time.
What creators never say
No "you will remember everything". No grades, scores or medical claims. Nothing your own pages do not say Speechify does.

Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.

Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run next to your team's), and a weekly look at downloads and trials.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Speechify" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Speechify themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Speechify does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
No medical claims
Creators speak about dyslexia, ADHD or reading only from their own experience. Nothing about treating or curing anything, and Speechify is described only in its own words.
Real pages, private details off
Only the creator's own reading on screen. No patient, client or classmate details, and only a few lines of any book or textbook at a time.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
