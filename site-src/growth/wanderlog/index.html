A growth breakdown for Wanderlog
Why we made this

Wanderlog has been planning trips since YC's Winter 2019 batch, and your home page sums it up: "Your itinerary and your map in one view." Your latest App Store notes add Import from anywhere, so a pasted link or a shared reel becomes an itinerary, and the listing holds 4.9 stars from 36K ratings with Editors' Choice.

01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
The reviews on your home page describe whole trips planned in Wanderlog. A traveler showing their own trip, day by day on the map, is that review in motion.
Show, do not tell
The map is the demo
Your home page says it: add a place and it pops up on your map. In a short video that happens in the first second.
Objections
Answer "my notes app works fine"
Every video ends on one plan in one view: the days, the map, the route and the costs. Viewers see what changes without being told.
A real moment
Holiday trips get planned now
Thanksgiving road trips, Christmas in New York and New Year trips are all being planned this month. Creators planning theirs on camera give viewers a reason to download Wanderlog the same week.
Fuel for ads
Creator videos become your ads
Each creator video doubles as a partnership ad. Eight openings per script means the budget goes where viewers actually stay.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like an itinerary
Travel TikTok already saves and shares itineraries, so people watch to the end instead of scrolling past. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not interrupt.
02 · What we learned
Where Wanderlog is today
The product
"One app for all your travel planning needs." Detailed itineraries, user-shared guides and your bookings, with your itinerary and your map in one view, color-coded by day. Route optimization, offline access, budgets and splitting costs, real-time collaboration, imported flight and hotel reservations, packing checklists and an AI Assistant. On iPhone, Android and the web.
Just shipped
Your latest App Store notes (version 2.227, 25 October): Import from anywhere, so you can "paste a link or share reels to automatically create an itinerary", plus refreshed visuals and enhanced maps. May to July: the new Trip Journal, to map trips, add photos, log stops and share them.
Your numbers
4.9 stars from 36K ratings on the US App Store and 4.7 from 37.7K reviews on Google Play, with Editors' Choice on both. 8M+ trips planned and over 1 million people who have tried Wanderlog, from your home page.
Already in motion
  • Travel bloggers can embed Wanderlog maps in their own posts, from your "How to embed a map on your travel blog" page.
  • User-shared guides on your home page, from a Paris 5-day itinerary to a Boston walking tour.
  • Press picks from The New York Times, WIRED, The Guardian, Condé Nast Traveler and Android Authority.

The timing

04 · The objection
"My notes app and Google Maps already work": the objection to win
Most people already plan in a notes app, a spreadsheet and a list of saved pins. The videos should answer with the same trip in one view: the days, the map, the route and the costs, shared with everyone coming.
What you already say
  • Your itinerary and your map in one view
  • No more switching between different apps, tabs, and tools
  • Add a place, and it'll pop up on your map
  • Track your route and easily export it to Google Maps
  • Invite your trip mates and plan your upcoming trip together in real-time
Keep Google Maps
Nobody has to give up Google Maps: your App Store page says Pro exports the trip to it. In a video that is one shot, the plan opening in Google Maps at the trailhead.
One trip, one view
Every script starts from a real list of places and ends on one plan a viewer can name: a city break, a road trip, a group trip or next year's trips.
How we use it
Script A plans a holiday city trip in one view. Script B maps a road trip with drive times. Script C gets a group trip out of the group chat. Script D turns saved reels into 2027 plans.
Keep it honest
Real trips only: the creator's own places, dates and bookings. Features are shown the way they work on the posting day, with Pro switched on where a feature needs it.
What creators never say
No promises about saving money or hours in numbers. Nothing your own pages do not say Wanderlog does. No booking codes, confirmation emails or tripmates' details on screen.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Wanderlog" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Wanderlog themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Wanderlog does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Real trips only
Every plan on screen is the creator's own trip, with their own places and dates. Features are shown the way they work on the posting day, and nothing is said about Wanderlog that your pages do not say.
Adults only, private details off screen
Every creator is 18+, and so is everyone named or shown in a group trip. Booking codes, confirmation emails, addresses and tripmates' details are blurred or kept off screen, and no phone is used on camera while driving.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
