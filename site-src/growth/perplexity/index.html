04 · The objection
"Isn't that for research?": the objection to win
AI roundups already file Perplexity under research. The videos should show the everyday side: a question asked out loud, a gift list, a trip, and Comet doing a chore.
What you already promise
  • Answers to any question, backed by sources and citations
  • Voice: answers whether you type or say it
  • Product cards with reviews from across the web, and Instant Buy in the US
  • Comet: the browser that works for you
Say it, see the sources
Your App Store page lists voice and cited sources for every answer. A question asked out loud at the game, with the sources on screen, shows both in one shot.
Show it doing the task
Comet's page lists wrangling inboxes, ordering groceries and planning vacations among the things to hand over. A screen recording of Comet doing one real chore is the proof.
How we use it
Script A asks out loud, Script B builds a gift list, Script C plans a holiday trip, and Script D hands Comet a chore. Every one is a real question from the creator's own week.
Keep it real
Real questions, real lists and real trips, on the creator's own account, with answers and product cards shown exactly as they appear.
What creators never say
Nothing your own pages do not say Perplexity or Comet does. No money, health, legal or political questions. No offers that are not live on the day.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Perplexity" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Perplexity themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Perplexity does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Everyday questions only
Creators ask about gifts, trips, sports, books, plans and chores. No money, health, legal or political questions on camera, and every answer is shown exactly as it appears.
Private details off
Only the creator's own lists, inboxes and accounts on screen, with names, addresses, card details and other people's messages hidden.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
