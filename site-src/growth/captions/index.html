02 · What we learned
Where Captions is today
The product
Captions is an AI video generator and video editor that makes it easy to go from idea to ready-to-share content, no experience needed, in your App Store's words. AI Edit cuts scenes, overlays B-roll and adds music in a style you pick, a chat-based editor takes plain-language changes, and quick fixes cover eye contact, background noise, pauses and filler words. Plus auto captions, subtitles in 100+ languages, AI Dubbing, the AI Teleprompter and AI avatars, on iPhone, Android and the web.
Just shipped
28 October: version 13.41.0 of the iPhone app. Since September: Tesseract, your creative engine for AI agents, in Claude and ChatGPT, with HDR video (15 October) and any video as a reference (28 October). 04 · The objection
"I'm awkward on camera": the objection to win
Most people who should be on video say it first: talking to a camera feels awkward. The videos should answer with a real take: the script on the teleprompter, the glance at the notes fixed, the pauses trimmed, and the person still sounding like themselves.
What you already promise
  • Read your script on the AI Teleprompter, which keeps pace with your voice
  • "Glance at your notes, lose the camera, restart your thought. You can fix it all later."
  • Trim pauses, filler words and other interruptions
  • Polished, but never robotic: expressions and intentional pauses stay
The feeling already travels
"talking to a camera felt SO awkward when i first started" passed 19.8K plays, "habits for talking on camera" 184.8K, and a bakery owner's "suddenly you've forgotten how to be a person" 57.4K.
Same person, on camera
Your eye contact page says it preserves your expressions and behaviors, so you'll always look natural. That is a shot, not a sentence: the raw take and the edit side by side, same face, same words.
How we use it
Script B is the awkward take fixed on camera: teleprompter first, then Eye Contact. Script C follows a camera-shy owner from day 1. Scripts A and D put the raw take next to the edit.
Keep it real
Real raw takes, never made worse on purpose, and the Captions edit exported exactly as the app made it on the day.
What creators never say
No "you will go viral". No promises about followers, bookings or sales. Nothing your own pages do not say Captions does.

How the paid ads would run
1 · Film
Each script is shot once, with 8 different openings.
→
2 · Launch
Each opening runs as its own ad, from the creator's handle as a partnership ad.
→
3 · Read
After a few days we compare cost per download and how many people watch past 3 seconds.
4 · Cut
The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
The App Store and Google Play, or captions.ai for web sign-ups, with one tracking link per creator, so every download traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run next to your team's), and a weekly look at downloads and trials.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Captions" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Captions themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Captions does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Real takes only
Every "before" is the creator's real raw take, never made worse on purpose, and every "after" is exported from Captions exactly as the app made it on the day. No mocked-up screens.
Faces and consent
Only the creator's own face and voice. If an AI avatar or AI Twin ever appears, the video says so on screen, and customers in shop videos agree in writing first.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
