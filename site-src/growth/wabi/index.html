01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people

Show, do not tell

Wabi's moment is the second a message becomes a map, a vote or a potluck list. A short clip is exactly where that moment lands.
Objections
Answer "what can it see?" on camera
Everyone wonders what an AI in a group chat can see. A real group showing Wabi checking before it acts answers that faster than any policy page.
A real moment

Fuel for ads

Doesn't feel like an ad

02 · What we learned
Where Wabi is today
The product
The AI assistant you use together. Bring your partner, family, friends or coworkers into a Wabi chat, and it plans, books, handles the boring stuff and makes the app the moment needs: a map and itinerary for a trip, a shared calendar for a family week, a vote for a decision, a tracker for a goal. On iPhone, Android and Mac, with home screen widgets.
Just shipped
28 September: Wabi 2.0, "a new kind of messenger that makes apps and gets things done for you and your friends". 6 and 14 October: Android, then Mac. 19 October: open to everyone. 20 October: home screen widgets. 22 October: Plaid, for managing money with your partner or friends.

04 · The objection
"What can it see?": the objection to win
Every group chat asks the same thing first: what can it see? The videos should answer it before anyone asks.
What you already promise
  • You choose what Wabi can access
  • Wabi checks with you before important actions
  • Disconnect any connection whenever you want
  • Access is scoped to each chat, and everyone in it can see when personal information is requested
Everyone sees the same plan
Your store page puts it simply: everyone can see the same plan and move it forward. That is a shot, not a sentence: the vote, the app and the approval, all inside one chat.
Audited, and your data is not sold
Wabi is SOC 2 compliant and keeps most user data in the U.S., as Eugenia told TechCrunch on 19 October. Your privacy choices page says Wabi does not sell personal information for money.
How we use it
Script A shows the moment Wabi checks first and the creator approves. Scripts A and B show the whole group seeing the same plan in one chat, and every script films a chat the whole group agreed to show.
Consent on camera
Everyone in a filmed chat is 18+ and says yes before we film. Names, numbers and messages from anyone else stay blurred.
What creators never say
No "it reads everything". No promises about money saved, prices or bookings. Nothing your own pages do not say Wabi does.

06 · The moment
From open launch to the holidays
Wabi opened to everyone on 19 October. Friendsgiving and Thanksgiving come next, then holiday trips and gift exchanges: the busiest group chat weeks of the year.
Now to mid-November
6 to 8 creators film Scripts A and B: a weekend trip and a Friendsgiving potluck, each planned in one Wabi chat. The first partnership ads go live on Meta and TikTok.
Friendsgiving to Thanksgiving (21-26 Nov)
Hosts post the potluck list as guests fill it in, and roommates film Script C. Budget moves to the hooks that win.
December
Trips home, gift exchanges and New Year plans, each made in a group chat. The best clips keep running as ads into January.
What this needs from you
  • Creators set up Wabi with their real friends, partner or roommates a few days before filming
  • A tracking link per creator to wabi.ai/download, the App Store or Google Play
  • One person on your team who signs off within 48 hours
Why not wait for January?
Group plans peak now. Videos filmed in November are tested and cut before the holiday rush, not after it.

How the paid ads would run
1 · Film
Each script is shot once, with 8 different openings.
→
2 · Launch
Each opening runs as its own ad, from the creator's handle as a partnership ad.
→
3 · Read
After a few days we compare cost per download and how many people watch past 3 seconds.
4 · Cut
The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
wabi.ai/download, the App Store and Google Play, with one tracking link per creator, so every download traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (your privacy choices page already lists Meta, TikTok and Snap as ad partners), and a weekly look at downloads.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Wabi" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Wabi themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Wabi does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Consent in the chat
Everyone in a filmed chat is 18+ and agrees to be shown. Names, numbers, emails and money details of anyone else are blurred or left out.
Only real results
Every app on screen is one Wabi made in that chat. No promises about money saved, prices or bookings beyond what your own pages say.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
