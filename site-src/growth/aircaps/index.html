Objections

Relatable

02 · What we learned
Where AirCaps is today
The product
Lightweight smart glasses (49g) with a private display in the lenses. Four microphones pick up the people near you, and AirCaps shows what they say as captions in about 300ms, translates 60+ languages, and takes meeting notes with speaker labels. Built for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community, travellers and professionals.
The offer
HSA/FSA eligible, with free US shipping, 15-day returns and a 1-year warranty. Works without a subscription: captions in 9 languages are free forever, and Pro adds 60+ languages and AI notes. Prescription lenses fit, and your own guide says the glasses can be worn alongside hearing aids and cochlear implants.
Your own channels
Your YC page says your early TikToks passed 75M views. Since July, Deaf creators have filmed AirCaps on TikTok, among them @recklessperez75, @stefan.el.anderson, @malcanthearyou and @taleswithkails. On X you are shipping speech models in public: A5S V2 on 21 September and Diarization-v1 on 12 October.
Already in motion
  • TranscribeGlass became AirCaps in October 2025, adding translation and meeting notes to captions.
  • Your site maps the moments that matter: the theatre, a doctor's appointment, family dinner, a Netflix binge, the classroom.
  • Your shop page says 97% would recommend AirCaps to a friend and 90% use it daily.
  • The Yale Alumni Magazine (March/April 2026) tested AirCaps at a movie and on a noisy walk across campus.
What that tells us
AirCaps sells when someone shows one real moment: the joke caught at dinner, the movie without a caption device. Your reviews and your first creators already follow that pattern. What is missing is volume: more faces, in more of those moments, every week.
The timing
The holidays bring the biggest family tables of the year: Thanksgiving is on 26 November, then December's dinners and parties. FSA money generally has to be used within the plan year (HealthCare.gov), and AirCaps is HSA/FSA eligible.

04 · The objection
"Will it keep up at a real table?": the objection to win
Before anyone buys caption glasses, they ask: will it keep up in a loud room, with three people talking at once? The videos should answer that on camera.
What you already promise
  • 97% caption accuracy and 300ms latency
  • Four microphones with beamforming, built for noisy restaurants and parties
  • Speaker identification, so you see who said what
  • Worn alongside hearing aids, cochlear implants and prescription lenses
  • Captions in 9 languages, free forever, no subscription
The proof you already have
4.9 stars from 127 reviews, and your shop page says 97% would recommend AirCaps to a friend and 90% use it daily. Your reviewers write about following the table at dinner, and a whole movie in a theatre with no caption device.
The most believable shot
The view through the lens at a loud table, unedited, with the room's noise left in. Your site already films demos this way; creators can do it at their own dinners.
How we use it
Script A films a real family dinner through the lens. Script C follows one creator through a real week. Every script keeps the room's noise in the audio.
On camera
AirCaps captions the people nearby, so everyone at the table is an adult who knows and agrees on camera first. No strangers' faces or conversations.
What creators never say
No health or medical claims. No numbers your own pages do not publish. Nothing your pages do not say AirCaps does.

The brief

Where: Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels as Meta partnership ads to the shop page (15s and 30s cuts), plus the full master on TikTok (as a Spark Ad) and YouTube Shorts

Length: 35-40s master. 30s cut: shots 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 8. 15s cut: shots 1, 2, 5 and 8 (hook, disclosure, the joke that lands, CTA).

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with AirCaps" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use AirCaps themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say AirCaps does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Consent at the table
AirCaps captions the people nearby, so everyone on camera or in the captions is an adult who knows and agrees on camera first. No strangers, no children, no private conversations.
In their own words
Creators describe their own hearing in their own words, and nobody else's. No health or medical claims, and nothing your own pages do not say AirCaps does.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
