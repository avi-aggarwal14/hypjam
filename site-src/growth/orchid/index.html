01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
A founder saying "it drafts my replies" is a claim. A realtor approving a drafted follow-up in her car between showings is proof.
Show, do not tell
A text thread needs a demo
There is no app to screenshot. The product is the moment a text arrives with the work already done, and a short video shows that in ten seconds.
Objections
Answer "is it safe?" on camera
Everyone wonders what it can see. A real person showing the approval tap and the disconnect button does more than a safety page.
Paid only
Show the value before the first text
With no free tier, people need to see what they are paying for. Creators showing their own week with Orchid do exactly that.
Fuel for ads
Your best ads come from creators
The strongest creator videos become your Meta ads. We test 8 openings per script, so the budget follows what people actually watch.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like a normal post
So people actually watch it instead of scrolling past. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not feel like one.

04 · The objection
"It reads my email": the objection to win
Before anyone texts an AI their inbox, they ask two things: is it safe, and do I even need it? The videos have to answer both.
What you already promise
  • Google sign-in, so no password is ever shared
  • Drafts stay drafts until you say send
  • Calendar holds wait for your okay
  • Never sold, shared or used to train a model
  • Disconnect or delete everything, any time
What people search first
Your own guide says people search "Orchid AI review", "reddit" and "is it safe" before they text you. Creator videos are where those answers get seen.
"Who needs an AI for that?"
The other question in the replies to your 28 July launch was who needs an assistant to move a meeting. The answer is a day that is obviously full, so every script casts people whose time is clearly worth more than $29.99 a month.
How we use it
Every script shows the approval tap on camera. Script C hands "is it safe?" to a working EA. Scripts A and B cast people whose days are obviously full, so the help never looks lazy.
On camera
Only demo inboxes or blurred screens. No client, colleague or family names, emails or numbers, unless that person agrees.
What creators never say
Never "it sends emails on its own" or "it's free". Never that it replaces a person. Nothing your site does not say Orchid does.

16 · Ads
How the paid ads would run
1 · Film
Each script is shot once, with 8 different openings.
→
2 · Launch
Each opening runs as its own ad, from the creator's handle as a partnership ad.
→
3 · Read
After a few days we compare cost per new subscriber and how many people watch past 3 seconds.
4 · Cut
The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→
5 · Remix
Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→
6 · Refresh
New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
A link or first-text keyword per creator that opens Messages with a ready-made first text, the way your Notion Mail switch page already does. Every new subscriber traces back to the video that drove it.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Access to your Meta ad account (or we run it from ours), a way to see new subscribers by link or keyword, and a weekly look at the numbers.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Orchid" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Orchid themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Orchid does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Screens and consent
Only demo or blurred inboxes on camera. No client, colleague or family names, emails or numbers unless that person agrees.
Approval on camera
Every action that leaves the thread is shown being approved first, because that is how Orchid works. Nobody says it acts on its own.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
