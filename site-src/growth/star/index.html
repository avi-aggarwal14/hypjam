02 · What we learned
Where Star is today
The product
Star builds real games from a description. You describe what the player does, Star builds a first version you can play in a few minutes, and you change it with follow-up instructions like 'add enemies' or 'make it faster'. Ask for multiplayer and Star hosts the rooms; ask for a leaderboard and players get a score to beat. Games run in the browser in 2D or real 3D, publish to Explore with a link friends open without installing anything, and export to itch.io, Windows or your own files. Sliders tune gravity, speed and spawn rates while the game runs, and AI playtesting is on by default.
Just shipped
17 October: the Halloween Update. Nova multiplayer gives each game its own server, built and run by Star, plus character voices, a soundtrack from a description, game scenes turned into cinematics, map editors and balance panels inside your game, Star for coding agents, cloud saves, and Clip, now rolling out, which turns up to the last 30 seconds of play into a video. 2 October: reuse art, sounds or code from your other games. September: AI playtesting, player insights and Autopilot.
Your numbers
42,800+ games made on Star (your home page). Six community game jams since May, the latest the Halloween Jam from 17 to 31 October. Corn Clicker is currently the #1 game on Star, as your getting-started guide puts it. YC S22.
Already in motion
  • Your For Creators page already lists the content: challenge videos, leaderboard features, reaction content, creator battles and short clips of the best runs for TikTok and Shorts.
  • Your community ships real games: DEEPSMOKE by crwlspaceINTERACTIVE on your home page, and Hell County, the car combat game you built with the Halloween Update.
  • Your channels: @BuildWithStar and @RyanEndacott on X, the Star Discord for jams, feedback and playtests, and docs with video tutorials.
What that tells us
Star is made for video: one sentence becomes a playable game in minutes, a link puts friends in it, and Clip turns the best run into a video. What is missing is volume: everyday US and UK creators showing it every week, from the group chat to the stream.

Thanksgiving is 26 November and Black Friday the day after, then the holiday break: friends back together, group chats full of new inside jokes, and time to make something. Content started now has a month to find what works.

04 · The objection
"I could never make a game": the objection to win
Most people have never made a game and assume it takes code, an engine and months. The videos should answer with a demo: one sentence typed, a game playing a few minutes later, and a friend opening the link.
What you already promise
  • No coding experience: describe what the player does
  • A first version usually takes a few minutes
  • Play it, then tell Star what to change, like 'add enemies' or 'make it faster'
  • Friends open it in their browser without installing Star
  • Drag sliders to adjust gravity, speed and spawn rates while the game runs
The habit already exists
People already make things for their friends: birthday roasts, inside jokes, group chat lore. Your For Friends page puts it best: the more specific the inside joke, the funnier the game.
Show the first version
Your getting-started guide says it: build the core loop first, then edit one thing at a time. That is a shot list, not a sentence: type, play, change one thing, play again.
How we use it
Script A films one group chat game start to finish. Script B makes a game live for a stream chat. Script C is one sentence and three changes. Script D turns a holiday jam into a series.
Keep it real
The creator's own idea typed on screen, the real game Star built, 'a few minutes later' on every cut, and friends' real reactions.
What creators never say
Star shown the way your pages describe it: a game editor with hosting, multiplayer rooms, leaderboards and exports. Games are always shown as made with Star, never as hand-coded, as your terms ask. No pricing or earnings talk.

06 · The moment
From Thanksgiving to the holiday break
Thanksgiving is 26 November and Black Friday the day after. Then the holiday break: friends and family together, group chats full of new inside jokes, and time to make something. Videos filmed in November are tested before that rush.
Now to Thanksgiving (26 Nov)
6 to 8 creators film Script A about a real friend group joke and Script C, one sentence to a game. The first partnership ads go live on Meta and TikTok.
Black Friday to the break
Streamers film Script B for their holiday streams, and creators make winter games for their friends. Budget moves to the hooks and creators that bring sign-ups.
The holiday break
Script D jam series run over the break, with voices, music and a map editor. The best November clips keep running as ads into the New Year.
What this needs from you
  • A Star account for each creator, ready to build from day one
  • A tracking link per creator to buildwithstar.com
  • Which features can appear in paid videos, for example Nova multiplayer, voices, music or Clip
  • One person on your team who signs off within 48 hours
Why not wait for the New Year?
The holiday break is when people have time to make something for their friends. Videos filmed in November are tested and cut by then, so the budget backs proven hooks, not first drafts.

The weakest openings switch off and the budget moves to the best two.
→

Winning hooks get new bodies and new creators.
→

New creator videos every week, so the ads never go stale.
Where the ads point
buildwithstar.com, with one tracking link per creator, so every sign-up traces back to the video that drove it. Videos can also link the creator's own published game, so viewers play before they sign up.
Why partnership ads
Ads that run from a creator's own handle look like that creator's post, not a brand ad. That is the format these videos are built for.
What we would need
Each creator's OK for partnership ads, access to your Meta and TikTok ad accounts (or ad sets we run next to your team's), and a weekly look at sign-ups and games published.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Star" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Star themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Star does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.
Star in your words
A game editor that builds real games from a description, with hosting, multiplayer rooms, leaderboards and exports, described only as your pages describe it. Every game is shown as made with Star, never as hand-coded.
Kind and private
Friends and viewers appear only if they agree, inside jokes stay kind, and no last names, numbers or private messages on screen. No gambling-style mechanics or prizes in any game we film.
How we run it
Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
