A growth breakdown for Sesame
Why we made this

You built Sesame around a simple idea: talking to a computer should feel like a real conversation, with timing and rhythm, not prompts. Your research preview drew more than a million people in its first few weeks, as Sequoia wrote when it backed your Series B. 01 · Why UGC
Why creator videos work
Authenticity
People trust people
Your App Store page says Sesame builds personal agents for curious people. A real person asking a real question on their walk, and getting a real answer back, is proof no feature list can match.
Show, do not tell
The demo is a conversation
One press of the Action button, a question, and a search card with images appears while the agent keeps talking. Ten seconds of that shows timing and rhythm better than any description.
Objections
Answer "talking to AI feels weird" on camera
Many people have only ever given a voice assistant a command. A creator chatting naturally in the middle of a walk answers that better than any FAQ.
A real moment
The holidays and the New Year
Trips to plan, gifts to research and New Year projects to start. Each one is a reason to call Maya, Miles, Simone or Charlie, and a story to film.
Fuel for ads
Creator videos become your ads
Each creator video doubles as a partnership ad. Eight openings per script means the budget goes where viewers actually stay.
Doesn't feel like an ad
Looks like a normal walk-and-talk
So people watch, save and comment with their own questions. It is still labelled as an ad, it just does not interrupt.
02 · What we learned
Where Sesame is today
The product
Sesame makes personal agents for curious people. Maya is a witty collaborator with boundless imagination, Miles a laid-back conversationalist who tells it like it is, Simone a dynamic thought partner with a knack for deep-dives, and Charlie a casual sounding board who helps you work things out. You call or text them. They search the web while they talk, show search cards with images, create notes, set reminders and summarize takeaways, and each one has its own memory. On iOS and Android, with glasses coming in 2027, Made in Japan.
Just shipped
27 May: the iOS preview in 39 countries, with Simone and Charlie joining Maya and Miles, plus Deep Dives, texting and Incognito Mode. 5 and 6 October: a new voice model, voice assistants for everyone on iOS and Android, each agent with its own computer, a few Google apps and your own MCPs to connect, skills you build by talking, and glasses that will be Made in Japan. 27 October: version 46 on the App Store.
Your numbers
1M+ people talked to Maya and Miles in the research preview's first few weeks, and they generated 5M+ minutes of conversation (Sequoia, October 2025). A $250M Series B with Sequoia and Spark (TechCrunch, October 2025), after a Series A led by a16z (February 2025). 4.9 stars on the US App Store.
Already in motion
  • A UK creator's "Having chat with sesame a new AI voice model" passed 947K plays (March 2025).
  • Creators kept filming Sesame this year, from first tests in February to "the most realistic sounding AI I have ever heard" in June.
  • Your channels: @sesame on X, @madebysesame on Instagram, your Discord and r/MadeBySesame on Reddit, plus a live Reddit AMA with your design, product and engineering leads in October.
What that tells us
The voice works on camera: people film their first chat because it surprises them, and those videos travel. What is missing is volume: everyday US and UK adults using Maya, Miles, Simone and Charlie for something real, a question on a walk, an idea, a plan, every week.
The timing
Thanksgiving is 26 November and Black Friday the day after. December brings trips and gifts to research, and January is when people start the project they have been circling. Content started now has a month to find what works.

04 · The objection
"Talking out loud to an AI feels weird": the objection to win
Most people have only ever given a voice assistant a command, so talking to one in public sounds strange. The videos should answer with a demo: a real person mid-walk, talking the way they talk to anyone, and an agent keeping up.
What you already promise
  • Ultra-low latency conversation partners that know when to respond, when to listen and when to get out of the way
  • Just speak your mind instead of agonizing over the perfect prompt
  • Texting for when it is easier not to speak out loud
  • Incognito Mode for conversations you want to keep private
The habit already exists
Walk-and-talks, commute routines and voice notes are everyday content. "Your best ideas show up on a walk, and by Friday they're gone" is how one US creator put it on 30 October.
Talk first, on camera
Your getting-started guide puts it simply: start a call, bring whatever is on your mind, and figure it out together. That is a shot, not a sentence: one press of the Action button and a creator mid-stride.
How we use it
Script A films one real walk, start to finish. Script C puts the same question to all four characters. Script D turns curiosity into a daily series.
Keep it real
Real conversations recorded as they happen, the creator's own question, and the agent's real replies, search cards and notes on screen.
What creators never say
Sesame shown the way you describe it: a partner for curiosity, ideas and getting things done, within your own guidelines on what the assistants are for. No health claims, and nothing about the glasses beyond your own words.

06 · The moment
From Thanksgiving to New Year plans
Thanksgiving is 26 November and Black Friday the day after. December brings trips, gifts and holiday questions, and January is when people start the project they have been circling. Videos filmed in November are tested before that rush.
Now to Thanksgiving (26 Nov)
6 to 8 creators film Script A on their real walks and Script C with all four characters. The first partnership ads go live on Meta and TikTok.
Black Friday to the holidays
Rabbit-hole series (Script D), plus trips and gifts planned out loud. Budget moves to the hooks and creators that bring downloads.
The New Year
Script B for the project people promise themselves in January, with a Monday skill to keep it going. The best November and December clips keep running as ads.
What this needs from you
  • A Sesame account for each creator, ready to film from day one
  • A tracking link per creator to the App Store or Google Play
  • Which characters, voices and features can appear in paid videos
  • One person on your team who signs off within 48 hours
Why not wait for January?
People set New Year goals in the last week of December and try new apps on 1 January. Videos filmed in November are tested and cut by then, so the budget backs proven hooks, not first drafts.

19 · Rules
Rules we never break
Disclosure
"Ad | Paid partnership with Sesame" on screen, said in the first seconds, and the platform's paid-partnership label on. That is what the FTC asks for.
Who films
Adults only (18+). Creators use Sesame themselves before they talk about it.
What gets said
Nothing your own pages do not say Sesame does. No invented numbers. We never pay for a positive opinion, only for the video.

Every video reviewed within 48 hours before it posts. Creators paid within 7 days of going live.
