# Content audit — `content/*.json`

Scope: all sixteen files in `content/`, read in full, checked against CONTRACT §3, §7–§13 and `ref/` (outlines.txt, index.html, hero_dom.html, hire.json/join.json/studio.json in hypjam-brand). Read-only audit; nothing in `content/` was edited except this file. Mechanical checks (forbidden words, spellings, hype, percentages, brand-name census, href resolution, asset existence, schema keys, word counts, camera maths, cross-file identity) were run by script; honesty and voice were judged by reading.

Severity: **block** = must change before build; **fix** = should change before launch; **nit** = polish.

**Verdict: MAJOR.** One block remains (blog `link_map` no longer resolves the `/hire` tokens in the article bodies). Beyond that the hard §10 lines hold — no invented client, testimonial, review, metric, certification or "trusted by" — but a cluster of honesty fixes needs the owner: nine pages imply an existing client base ("Brands on hypjam…"), fifteen first-person quotes are attributed to Avi Aggarwal without his sign-off, a physical London shoot bay and photographs "of hypjam's HQ" are asserted, and a dozen operational promises were invented beyond hire.json. Two seal files are missing, and the pages carried over from hypjam-brand are in a different register from Lance's.

**Live edits during the audit.** At 22:00 nine files changed under me (nav, footer, home, faq, process, rights, solutions, hero, blog). I re-checked the current state: every `/services/` href site-wide now uses services.json's short slugs, nav/footer now point at `/solutions/dtc-ecommerce`, and the hero marquee paths now match the shipped SVGs — three findings I had rated block/fix are resolved and appear below as residue nits (#12, #13) or in §3. The blog edit re-keyed `link_map` from `/hire` to `/book` and created #1.

## 2. What passed

- **Forbidden words** — no `hotel`, `guest`, `housekeeping`, `front desk`, `Lance`, `AI agent`, `AI-powered`, `revolutionary` or `trusted by` in any visible string. `studio` appears only inside asset file names (#25). The stop ids `1-front-of-house` etc. are engine ids mandated by §8, not copy.
- **Honesty, hard lines** — no invented client, testimonial, review, case study, outcome metric, percentage-as-result, real company as customer, certification or "trusted by". Every reserved slot uses the §10 strings exactly ("The first brand quote goes here.", "The first brand story goes here.", "Filled in at launch", "reserved"). Sample tiles on /work are labelled `illustrative` with the disclosure intro kept. Percentages found are safe-zone fractions (top 15%/bottom 20%) and mock-spec fills, not results. "Our practice, not a certification" is stated on the ASA/CAP item. Brand-name census found none of ~90 real brands; the capitalised-token census shows only platforms, the founder, London/Leeds and the disclosed invented sample brands.
- **British English** — colour/organise/authorise/labelled/anonymised/scepticism throughout; the -ize scan found only "resized"; the one "meter" is "level meter" in a mock spec. `licence`/`license` correct in 62 of 65 places (#14).
- **Sentence case** — every h1/h2/label/eyebrow is sentence case (only #16 is Title Case). "hypjam" is lowercase in all 800+ occurrences, including sentence starts. No exclamation marks; no straight double quotes in prose.
- **Home length parity vs Lance (index.html + hero_dom.html)** — trust 19w/121c vs 18w/132c; HUD descriptions 14/12/17/12/12 vs 14/12/17/11/10; features heading 4 vs 4, sub 18 vs 17, descs 9–10 vs 9–10; rights heading 6 vs 6, sub 13 vs 11, items 18–19 vs 14–21; metrics sub 7 vs 7, labels 8/5/8 vs 8/4/5; stories heading 4+5 vs 4+5; founder quote 38 vs Lance's 37; footer headline 6 vs 6. - **Services/solutions parity** — page h1s 7–11 words vs Lance 8–10 (except #20); bullet titles 1–8 vs 3–7; steps, comparison values (1–4 words), problem titles and FAQ questions in range; every service page has 6 features × 3 bullets, 4 problems, 4 steps, 6 comparison cells, 6 FAQs, one quote; every goal page has 3 facts, 4 services-behind, 4 features, 4 problems, 4 steps, 6 hood cells, 5 FAQs, 3 related; every brand page has 3 facts, 4 fits, a band, 4 steps, 3 FAQs, 3 related and a reserved quote — matching Lance's section anatomy field for field.
- **Completeness (§3)** — content exists for /, /services (+9), /solutions (+9), /rights, /work, /blog (+8 articles, all body files present, every category in `filters`), /book, /process, /faq, /agency, /join, /privacy, /terms (both sources present, last-updated matches), /404.
